# Quick Task 261025-s0z: Flagged pre-browse onboarding + paywall (UI only)

Build a pre-browse onboarding and soft paywall for 零元 behind a default-off flag.
No payments, no schema changes, no deploy. Snag informs the interaction only;
everything in `/workspace/snag-research/ANALYSIS.md` §4 is rejected.

## Data shapes (chosen before logic)

- `lib/paywall/gate.ts`. Pure gate: `shouldShowOnboarding({ flagEnabled, forced, completed })`
  with `forced || (flagEnabled && !completed)`. Inputs come from `isPaywallFlagEnabled(env)`,
  `hasOnboardingForceParam(search)`, and `readOnboardingCompleted(storage)` on the versioned
  key `shoumanwu_onboarding_completed_v1`. `resolveOnboardingGate()` is SSR-safe (false without `window`).
- `lib/paywall/onboarding-flow.ts`. Reducer state machine over one `screen` union
  (`welcome | how | city | alerts | paywall | done`) plus `city` and `purchase: idle | comingSoon`.
  Actions: `next`, `back`, `skip`, `selectCity`, `startPurchase`, `finish`. No scattered booleans.
- `lib/paywall/plans.ts`. One config table of 3 plans (weekly, monthly, yearly) with
  `priceCents`, `periodWeeks`, `highlighted`, and `PLAN_PRICES_ARE_PLACEHOLDER = true`
  ("TODO: pending Guang's pricing decision"). Derived `perWeekCents()` and `formatPlanPrice()`.
- `lib/paywall/teaser.ts`. `summarizeCityTeaser(listings, city, limit)` reuses the feed's own
  `matchesConsumerCity` predicate, so the teaser count equals what the city filter shows.
  Source rows come from the existing `fetchActiveListingsClient()` (anon, read-only).
